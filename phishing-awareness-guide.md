# How to Spot a Phishing Email - A 2-Minute Guide

*Based on a real phishing email I investigated (Case 001). Written for non-technical staff.*

## What happened
An email claimed to be from the **Stellar Foundation**, offering free cryptocurrency
rewards for "staking" your assets. It looked legitmate and professionl, however it was a scam designed
to steal wallet details.

## 5 warning signs in this email

1. **The name and the address don't match**
   It said "Stellar Foundation", but the real address was `herb@southernheritagecc.com`,
   a company with nothing to do with Stellar.
   ➜ *Always check the actual email address, not just the name.*

2. **Too good to be true**
   "Earn rewards by staking your assets" is free money with no effort.
   ➜ *Real companies rarely email you unexpected rewards.*

3. **The link hides where it really goes**
   The button pointed to a Google link that secretly redirected to
   `stellarnewsletter.org`, a fake site made to look official.
   ➜ *Hover over a link (don't click) to see the real address.*

4. **A lookalike website**
   Stellar's real site is `stellar.org`. The scam used `stellarnewsletter.org`.
   ➜ *Small changes to a familiar name are a classic trick.*

5. **Pressure to act**
   Scams want you to act fast, before you think.
   ➜ *If it feels urgent, slow down.*

## What to do if you get an email like this

|                                         Do                                                   |                      Don't                          |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
|          Use the **Report phishing** button, or forward it to your IT/security team          |            Click links or open attachments          |
|                              Delete it after reporting                                       |                 Reply to the sender                 |
| Tell IT **straight away** if you clicked or entered a password; it's fixable if caught early | Feel embarrassed; reporting quickly is what matters |


## Remember
**Check the sender. Hover before you click. Report, don't reply.**
